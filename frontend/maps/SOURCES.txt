Derivation: retain features with iso_a2 == "US" (50 states and Washington,
DC); retain source geometry without simplification; use iso_3166_2 as the
feature id and properties.id; use BeenThere's geographic catalogue for
properties.name; omit other source properties; sort by ISO identifier.
Coordinates remain in WGS84 longitude/latitude. No runtime boundary service
or additional mapping library is needed for the US regional view.

These are generalized 1:50m cartographic boundaries, not legal or survey
boundaries. Country-coded US territories are not included in this state map.

Derivation: select iso_a2 AU or CA; retain only iso_3166_2 identifiers present
in BeenThere's catalogue (8 Australian states/territories; 13 Canadian
provinces/territories). Retain WGS84 geometry without simplification; set
feature id and properties.id to the catalogue code and properties.name to
the catalogue name. Omit other properties and sort by identifier.

DE.geojson and GB.geojson
Source: Natural Earth, 1:10m Admin 1 - States, Provinces, version 5.1.2.
Source URL:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson
License: Public domain. Attribution: Made with Natural Earth.
Derivation: Germany uses its 16 catalogue-matching iso_3166_2 identifiers.
For the UK, group all 232 iso_a2 GB features by their geonunit values:
England (152), Scotland (32), Wales (22), Northern Ireland (26). Map these
to GB-ENG, GB-SCT, GB-WLS and GB-NIR respectively. Repair source geometries
with Shapely make_valid and dissolve each constituent-country group with
union_all, removing internal local-authority borders. Germany and UK
geometries are simplified at 0.005 degrees with preserve_topology=True.
Use catalogue identifiers/names, omit source properties, sort by identifier.
Coordinates remain WGS84. Shapely is an offline derivation tool only, not a
Home Assistant runtime dependency. Generalized cartographic boundaries are
not legal or survey boundaries. Crown dependencies are not UK subdivisions.
